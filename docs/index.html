ONE MODEL · FIVE ROLES

A careful pipeline,
step by step.

01

Understand

Map every source column to a clear canonical schema.

02

Standardize

Normalize phones, email, dates, IDs, and booleans.

03

Unify meaning

Resolve equivalent majors, skills, roles, and tools.

04

Clean text

Remove noise while preserving the original answer.

05

Validate

Flag missing, duplicate, contradictory, or uncertain data.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN

Your data stays yours.

Qwen runs on your own computer. Direct identifiers are masked before inference, the model has no filesystem tools, and the original files are never modified.
